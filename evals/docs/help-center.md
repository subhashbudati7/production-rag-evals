# Refund Policy

Refunds are available within thirty days of purchase. Contact support with
your order number to start a refund. Digital downloads are refundable only
if unused. Shipping fees are not refundable.

# Shipping

Orders ship within two business days. Standard shipping takes five to seven
business days. Express shipping is available at checkout for an extra fee.

# Warranty

Hardware carries a one-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects.
The warranty does not cover accidental damage. To file a claim, register
your device and describe the defect.

# Account Security

Enable two-factor authentication to protect your account. Never share your
password. Support will never ask for your password by email.

# Order Tracking

You can track your order from the orders page in your account. A tracking
number is emailed within two business days of shipping. Contact support if
tracking has not updated after five days.

# Gift Cards

Gift cards never expire and never lose value. Check a gift card balance
from your account page. Gift cards cannot be exchanged for cash.

# Support Hours

Support is available Monday through Friday, nine to five Pacific time.
Email support at any time and expect a reply within one business day.

# International Shipping

We ship internationally to Canada and Mexico. International orders take
ten to fifteen business days. Customs fees are the buyer's responsibility.
